# Incident Response Policy

## 🎯 Purpose
This policy outlines how **Yirla** responds to security and availability incidents that may impact customers or their data. Our goal is to minimize disruption and maintain the highest standards of data integrity and transparency.

---

## 🔍 What We Consider an Incident
An incident includes, but is not limited to, the following events:
* **Unauthorized Access:** Any breach or suspected access to systems or customer data.
* **Service Outages:** Total service loss or severe performance degradation.
* **Data Integrity:** Issues affecting the accuracy, availability, or consistency of data.
* **Credential Compromise:** Suspected compromise of administrative credentials or core infrastructure.

---

## 📡 Detection & Assessment
Yirla utilizes a robust stack of monitoring, logging, and automated alerts to identify potential incidents. Upon detection, the response team evaluates:
1.  **Scope and Severity:** Is this a localized error or a platform-wide event?
2.  **Impact:** Which specific systems and customers are affected?
3.  **Containment:** What immediate actions are required to stop the spread or impact?



---

## 🛠 Response & Containment
Once an incident is confirmed, Yirla initiates the following protocol:
* **Containment:** Execute immediate technical steps to isolate affected systems.
* **Mitigation:** Implement workarounds or fixes to reduce customer impact.
* **Evidence Preservation:** Secure relevant logs, snapshots, and forensic evidence.
* **Restoration:** Return to normal service levels as quickly and safely as possible.

---

## 📢 Customer Communication
For incidents with verified customer impact:
* **Initial Notification:** Affected customers will be notified within a reasonable timeframe (typically within 24–48 hours for major security events).
* **Content:** Communications will include the known impact, mitigation steps taken, and anticipated next actions.
* **Updates:** Regular updates will be provided as material new information becomes available via designated channels.

---

## 📝 Post-Incident Review
Continuous improvement is core to our operations. After the resolution of any significant incident, Yirla conducts an internal review to:
* Identify the **Root Cause**.
* Implement **Corrective Actions** to prevent recurrence.
* Improve **Controls and Monitoring** systems.

*Note: Post-incident summaries (RCAs) may be shared with affected customers upon request for significant incidents.*

---
