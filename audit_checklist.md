# The LinkedIn "Wasted Spend" Audit Checklist (2026)
> Use this 10-point framework to identify and recover inefficient LinkedIn Ad spend.

## 1. The "Audience Network" Leak
**Check:** Is the LinkedIn Audience Network (LAN) enabled?
**The Risk:** For B2B, LAN often delivers high click volume but extremely low lead quality (junk traffic).
**Action:** Disable LAN or upload a strict exclusion list of mobile apps and non-business sites.

## 2. Job Title Over-Targeting
**Check:** Are you using more than 20+ Job Titles in a single campaign?
**The Risk:** Hyper-fragmenting titles drives up your CPC (Cost Per Click) significantly.
**Action:** Pivot to "Job Function" combined with "Seniority" to reach the same audience at a lower cost.

## 3. The "Member Group" Trap
**Check:** Are you targeting broad groups like "Marketing Professionals"?
**The Risk:** Many members join groups and never leave, making the data stale.
**Action:** Layer "Group Membership" with "Intent Signals" or "Recent Activity" to ensure the lead is currently active.

## 4. Location Mismatch
**Check:** Review the "Demographics" tab for Location.
**The Risk:** LinkedIn often shows ads to "Recently In" a location rather than "Permanently In."
**Action:** Ensure you are targeting "Permanent Residents" to avoid spending budget on travelers.

## 5. Creative Fatigue (The CDI Check)
**Check:** Is your Frequency above 4.0 in a 30-day window?
**The Risk:** Your Creative Decay Index ($CDI$) is likely peaking, meaning you are paying for impressions that no longer convert.
**Action:** Rotate in 3 new creative variations immediately.

## 6. The "Company Size" Filter
**Check:** Are you excluding "1-10" and "11-50" if you are an Enterprise seller?
**The Risk:** Smaller companies often have high engagement but zero budget authority for $25k+ deals.
**Action:** Set a hard floor on company size based on your ICP (Ideal Customer Profile).
