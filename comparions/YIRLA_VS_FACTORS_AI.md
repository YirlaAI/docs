# Yirla vs Factors.ai: Attribution Signals vs. Decision Judgment

## 🔍 The Attribution vs. Action Dilemma
When evaluating marketing intelligence tools, comparisons often blur. Attribution platforms, analytics tools, and AI copilots all claim to “optimize performance.” However, they solve fundamentally different problems. 

The core distinction: **Factors.ai explains credit; Yirla explains consequence.**



---

## 1. What Problem Does Each Product Solve?

### **Factors.ai (The Identity Layer)**
* **Primary Question:** *“Which accounts and channels influenced our revenue?”*
* **Core Function:** Connects anonymous web activity to known CRM accounts. It maps the buyer journey across touchpoints to determine ROI.
* **Key Focus:** Who engaged, when, and through which channel?

### **Yirla (The Decision Layer)**
* **Primary Question:** *“Given what we are running right now, what should we do next?”*
* **Core Function:** Detects performance decay, message saturation, and creative fatigue *before* the outcomes appear in a revenue report.
* **Key Focus:** What is failing, and where should the next dollar go?

---

## ⚖️ Attribution Signals vs. Decision Signals
The technical difference lies in the nature of the data signals each platform prioritizes.

| Feature | Factors.ai (Attribution) | Yirla (Judgment) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Data Nature** | Retrospective (Past) | Forward-Looking (Future) |
| **Key Metric** | Funnel Influence / Pipeline Credit | Creative Decay / Spend Efficiency |
| **Logic** | Identity Resolution & Mapping | Synthesis & Pattern Detection |
| **Goal** | Explain how revenue happened. | Prevent wasted spend. |



---

## 🏛 Position in the GTM Stack

### **Factors.ai is Downstream**
It lives alongside the CRM (Salesforce/HubSpot) and is most valuable to **RevOps and Revenue Leadership**. It answers board-level questions about which channels deserve more budget next year.

### **Yirla is Upstream**
It lives inside the Ad Platforms (LinkedIn/Google/Meta) and is most valuable to **Performance Marketing and Demand Gen**. It answers operational questions about which campaign needs to be paused *this Tuesday*.

---

## 🛠 Strategic Assumptions
* **Factors.ai assumes** that clean identity data and perfect CRM mapping are the keys to optimization.
* **Yirla assumes** that speed and actionable clarity matter more than theoretical completeness. We intentionally bypass attribution debates to focus on **Creative and Message resonance.**

---

## 🤝 Do They Compete?
While both sit under "Marketing Intelligence," they rarely compete at the decision level.
* **Factors.ai** justifies marketing spend *after* it has occurred.
* **Yirla** improves marketing performance *while* spend is flowing.

**Choose Factors.ai if:** You need to align sales and marketing on account-level ROI.
**Choose Yirla if:** You need to protect your $100k+ monthly budget from platform drift and creative fatigue.

---

## 🏁 Final Takeaway
Factors helps you understand **credit**. Yirla helps you understand **consequence**. One tells you who to thank; the other tells you what to change.
