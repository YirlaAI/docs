# Yirla Logic: Startup Segment (0-50 Employees)

## 🎯 Strategic Objective: Rapid Demand Capture
For the Startup segment, Yirla’s logic prioritizes **agility and immediate value signals**. Since startups have flat decision-making structures (1-3 stakeholders), our engine focuses on "Founder-Level" resonance and product-led growth (PLG) triggers.

## 🧠 Agentic Logic Rules
* **Decision Speed Multiplier:** Yirla weights engagement from Founders and CXOs at 3x, as they are typically the sole economic buyers.
* **Friction Level:** Logic favors "Low-Friction" CTAs (e.g., Free Trial, Ungated Playbook) to align with shorter sales cycles (30-90 days).
* **Creative Tone:** Automatically recommends "Visionary" or "Relatable" tones to build a personal connection with early-stage builders.

## 🚦 Targeting Parameters
* **Focus:** Firmographic data points for rapid growth, funding rounds, and "Technographic" shifts (e.g., adopting a new CRM).
* **Budget Logic:** Aggressive testing with smaller daily caps to find high-performing "Hooks" quickly.
