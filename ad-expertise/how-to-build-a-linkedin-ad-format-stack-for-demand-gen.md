Most LinkedIn format decisions happen by default: the creative team produces what it has capacity for, and that's what runs. That's inertia, not a format strategy, and inertia doesn't scale.

A deliberate format stack maps each format to a funnel stage with a specific job description:

| Format | Funnel Stage | Primary Job |
|---|---|---|
| Single image | Awareness | Fast messaging tests; cheap to produce |
| Document ad | Consideration | Deep engagement; 3–5x completion vs. gated PDF per [GrowthSpree's benchmark data](https://www.growthspreeofficial.com/blogs/linkedin-document-ads-b2b-2026) |
| Conversation ad | Bottom-funnel | High-intent moments like briefing requests |
| Carousel | ABM campaigns | Reach multiple buying committee roles in one placement |

Building this into your content pipeline means your campaign calendar should specify format alongside topic and audience, decided before creative production starts, not the week of launch. That's the operating system difference between "we ran some ads" and "we built a stack with a specific job at each stage."

The strategic case for ungating document ads is worth internalizing: the value at the consideration stage comes from account-level engagement signals, not lead capture.
