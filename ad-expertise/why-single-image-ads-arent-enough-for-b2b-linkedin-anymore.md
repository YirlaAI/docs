The gap between what your creative team is producing and what your buyers are responding to is probably wider than your last campaign report suggests.

[GrowthSpree's 2026 document ads analysis](https://www.growthspreeofficial.com/blogs/linkedin-document-ads-b2b-2026) found document ads delivering 3 to 5x the engagement of equivalent gated whitepaper campaigns, with view completion rates of 25 to 40%. The mechanism isn't complicated: document ads appear natively in feed without requiring a click-through-and-fill-a-form detour. Single image ads still have a place, they're fast to produce and cheap to test messaging on, but they can't carry the format mix alone anymore.

The format question is a positioning question. What you put in front of a VP signals whether you're investing in their time or just running ads. A stock photo with a generic headline says "we bought impressions." A document ad that teaches something useful in 90 seconds of scroll says something different.

Three things to drive this quarter:

1. Stack formats by funnel stage deliberately, single image for early awareness where you're testing messaging, document ads for consideration where engagement depth matters.
2. Default to ungated for document ads. The reach and engagement signal matter more than the lead capture for awareness-stage content.
3. Ask your team when they last tested a new format. "It still works" is not the same answer as "it's the best available option."
