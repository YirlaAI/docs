The standard whitepaper campaign flow — write it, gate it, promote it with a single image ad — is producing worse results than the same content would generate as an ungated document ad. The data is specific enough to act on.

[GrowthSpree's 2026 data](https://www.growthspreeofficial.com/blogs/linkedin-document-ads-b2b-2026) puts document ads at 3 to 5x the engagement rate of equivalent gated whitepaper campaigns, with 25 to 40% view completion on well-structured documents. The reason is friction: a document ad opens natively in feed without requiring a click, a landing page load, or a form fill. Remove the friction and more people actually read the content.

Here's the practical conversion from gated whitepaper to document ad campaign:

1. **Cut the content down.** Take your existing whitepaper and reduce it to a 6 to 10 slide document ad version, leading with your single most useful insight on slide one, not slide six.
2. **Run it ungated.** The engagement signal at the awareness and consideration stage is worth more than the lead capture. Gate the full-length version for retargeting later.
3. **A/B test against your current single image ad.** Same audience, same budget, same offer. Let CTR and view completion make the call instead of assumptions.
4. **Retarget with the gated version.** Audiences who completed 50%+ of the document ad are now warm. That's the right moment for the form fill, not the first impression.
